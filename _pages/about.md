I am an incoming PhD Student at the [Kempner Institute](https://kempnerinstitute.harvard.edu/) at Harvard. I completed my Bachelor's degree in  Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Pennsylvania, as well as a Master's degree in Mathematics. I am interested in geometric deep learning and equivariances in generative model design for computer vision, particularly in 3D and 4D reconstruction.

I am currently working in [GRASP](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) under the advisement of Professor [Kostas Daniilidis](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~kostas/) on generative modeling for camera pose estimation in ambiguous and symmetric settings.  Before this, I worked in the [Center for Particle Cosmology](https://particlecosmo.sas.upenn.edu/) under Professor [Bhuvnesh Jain](https://live-sas-physics.pantheon.sas.upenn.edu/people/standing-faculty/bhuvnesh-jain) on statistical methods for field analysis and dark matter reconstruction using generative methods. 

My thesis was on functional diffusion processes over the sphere under the advisement of Professor [Davi Maximo](https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~dmaxim/).

I am always happy to talk about research, so please feel free to reach out!
